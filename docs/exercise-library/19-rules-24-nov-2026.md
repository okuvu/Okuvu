# The worksheet rules of 24 November 2026

Status: **in force.** These govern every worksheet added from now on. They are
the owner's own words, and each one names the place in the code that enforces it
so a later change that breaks one fails a named test rather than quietly shipping.

Recorded from the owner's message of 24 Nov 2026 ("New tools needed"). Nothing
here changes an existing worksheet, the breathing card, the mood check-in, the
journal, chat, the Treasure Box or the safety plan: their wording and behaviour
are untouched, and the tests that pin them still pass.

Step report: `20-step-14-rules-report.md`.

## 1. Care plan labels

> Every worksheet can belong to one or more care plans: Anxiety & Worry, Low
> Mood, Stress & Burnout, or All. The client's assigned plan decides which
> worksheets show first on Home. The full library stays open to browse.

The labels, exactly as assigned:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Worry tree | Anxiety & Worry | `ws_worry_tree` |
| Spot the thinking trap | Anxiety & Worry | `ws_thinking_trap` |
| Thought record | Anxiety & Worry, Low Mood | `ws_thought_record` |
| Small things planner | Low Mood | `ws_small_things` |

| What I want to bring to session | All | `ws_bring_to_session` |
| Post-session takeaway | All | `ws_post_session` |
| My safety plan | All | `ws_safety_plan` |

**How it is enforced.**

- The keys are `anxiety_worry`, `low_mood`, `stress_burnout`, `all`; the labels
  above are the only client-facing-free spellings, and a typo is refused by a
  database `CHECK` and by the catalogue validator.
- The internal plan is still `CP1`/`CP2`/`CP3` (`CARE_PLAN_SCOPE_BY_ID`), and it
  is **never sent to the client**. The owner's earlier decision stands: the app
  never receives `care_plan_id` or a plan name. What the client experiences is
  order.
- `GET /library` puts the client's own plan first **inside each of the nine
  Library sections** (a stable partition per section — the app renders section by
  section, so a global sort would be undone on the way to the screen). Nothing is
  hidden, removed or greyed out: the full Library stays browsable.
- The Today card's one suggestion prefers the client's plan too, and stays
  deterministic (first matching row in the approved order), because a suggestion
  that changes every refresh reads as a notification to keep up with.
- A client with no plan yet is reordered for nobody.

Tests: `packages/shared-types/__tests__/worksheet-rules.test.ts` (rule 1) and
`apps/api/src/__tests__/worksheet-rules.test.ts` (ordering, and that the plan is
never serialized).

## 2. New worksheets stay hidden

> Every new worksheet I ask for from now on starts switched off for clients and
> cannot be assigned from the portal. Write draft wording for each one in plain,
> warm language and send it to me as a list to review. Only switch a worksheet on
> when I tell you its wording is approved.

- A worksheet's status is `draft` until the owner approves its wording. `draft`
  is the **column default**, so an insert that says nothing publishes nothing.
- A draft has a body and is previewable by a therapist; it is not a card, cannot
  be opened, and cannot be assigned.
- Client reads filter `status = 'approved'` in the query, so a draft cannot leak
  through a missed field in a response mapper. A direct open answers `404`
  exactly as an unknown id does.
- The portal's assignment picker offers approved rows only, and the assign call
  re-checks at the API (`loadAssignableTool`) rather than trusting the form.

The rules and the two pieces are in place; **no new worksheet wording has been
written yet**, because the message did not contain a list of new worksheets to
draft. When the list arrives, each draft comes back as a review list, and none of
it is switched on until the owner says the wording is approved.

## 3. Safety screening

> Every written or spoken answer in every new worksheet goes through the same
> safety check the journal uses today, with the same response: a gentle
> check-in, the helplines, the client's safety plan, and the therapist alert, all
> exactly as the journal does it. If the safety check is unavailable, treat it as
> moderate concern, never crisis, as we already do. Answers stay private by
> default with per-entry sharing, as now.

Already true of every worksheet, because there is one check and every write path
calls it (`apps/api/src/lib/safety-check.ts`): the deterministic crisis floor,
then the classifier under a deadline; a hit is the help card plus a flag with no
content plus the therapist alert; silence from the classifier is **moderate
concern** (S2), a gentle help offer, and a recheck — never a crisis. Voice answers
are screened too: only the transcript is stored, and it goes through the same
path.

What this step adds is the second sentence:

> For the Comparison check, Sleep diary, Meet the inner critic, Unsent letter and
> Doomscroll log, the check must also watch for: skipping meals, restricting food,
> making yourself sick, exercising to punish yourself, hating your body, not
> sleeping for days, wanting to sleep and not wake up, wanting to hurt someone,
> and self-harm.

- `apps/api/src/lib/worksheet-risk.ts` is that floor: deterministic, no model, and
  it can only ever **raise** the protective response.
- It is attached to the row (`ToolRegistryEntry.sensitive_check`), not to a list
  of ids in code, so a worksheet cannot gain it by being renamed or lose it by an
  id change. `'ws_comparison_check'`, `'ws_sleep_diary'`, `'ws_inner_critic'`,
  `'ws_unsent_letter'`, `'ws_scrolling_log'` are the five marked today.
- **Levels.** Self-harm and "wanting to sleep and not wake up" are **S4** (the
  crisis card). The eating, body, sleep and harm-to-someone signals are **S3** —
  flagged, so the client gets the help card with the helplines and their own
  safety plan and the therapist gets the content-free alert, exactly as a flagged
  journal entry does; but not the second-person crisis card, which would tell
  someone frightened of hurting somebody else to call a helpline about themselves.
  **This mapping is the clinical owner's call, not an engineer's** — it is
  implemented to match the existing S3/S4 meanings in `crisis-keywords.ts`, and
  it is listed in the step report for confirmation.
- Patterns are deliberately narrow: a single "I skipped breakfast because I was
  late" does not fire; "I've been skipping meals all week" does.

> None of these worksheets may ask for weight, body measurements, calories, food
> amounts or medicines.

Enforced on the body itself, not on the title: `worksheetRequestProblems()` reads
every client-facing string (prompt, helper, example, option label, feeling/part
label, column label, button label, intro, closing) and refuses a body that asks
for any of them. It runs on the save path (`worksheetRowProblems`), so a body that
got past a reviewer is still refused before it reaches a client.

## 4. Chat and worksheets

> When the chat helps a client with a worksheet, it may suggest which worksheet to
> try, encourage them, and ask open questions so the client finds their own words.
> It must never write an alternative or "balanced" thought for them, never say a
> thought is wrong or a thinking trap, and never fill in an answer. Show me the
> exact wording change to the chat's instructions before applying it, because I
> approve all chat wording.

**Nothing has been applied.** The exact change is written out in
`21-chat-wording-proposal.md`, for approval, and the chat's instructions are
unchanged until then.

The in-worksheet helper (`apps/api/src/agents/worksheet-help/prompts.ts`) already
carries the same rule — it may explain a step, encourage, and ask one open
question, and never writes the answer, the balanced thought or "another way to
see it", never names a thinking trap, and never sees the client's answers. That
part already holds; what rule 4 adds is the same promise for the chat's own
suggestions.

## 5. No scores

> No worksheet adds up answers into a total, a level or a label. Sliders show only
> the number the client chose.

- There is no aggregate piece type and no summing code: a scale stores one integer
  and renders that integer. `validateWorksheetAnswers` accepts a single number and
  refuses anything else.
- Copy that promises one ("your score is", "add them up", "your stress level") is
  refused by the same content check as rule 3, so a worksheet cannot tell a client
  their answers are a verdict even if it stores nothing.

## 6. The exposure ladder is therapist-only

> this one is therapist-only. It never appears in the client library, and the chat
> never suggests it.

- `access_mode = 'therapist_only'` is a first-class value. A row with it is
  filtered out of the Library query, answers `404` on a direct open, is refused on
  a save, and cannot be assigned.
- The chat never suggests worksheets by id (its toolkit list is empty in
  production), so there is no path from chat to a therapist-only row.
- `ws_exposure_ladder` exists as a placeholder with that access mode, so the rule
  is bound before the wording exists.

## 7. The two new pieces

> Some worksheets below need two pieces we don't have yet: a countdown timer, and
> a body outline the client can tap. Build these once, the same way as the other
> pieces.

Both are new piece types in the one shared definition
(`packages/shared-types/worksheet.ts`), so the api, the portal preview and the app
read the same object:

- **`countdown_timer`** — `seconds` (5–3600), optional `minSeconds`,
  `startLabel`/`doneLabel` from the approved copy. The answer is the number of
  seconds the timer ran. It is not a score, not compared to a threshold, and
  stopping early is a complete answer unless the piece sets a minimum.
- **`body_outline`** — `regions` (with optional one-level grouping), optional
  `maxSelections` (default 3) and `withStrength` (0–10 per part, the same scale as
  every other rating inside a worksheet). The words are the worksheet's; the app
  draws the figure. A part with no place on the drawing is still offered as a chip
  under it, so a copy change can never make a part unreachable.
- The portal preview renders both (the timer as its length, the outline as its
  parts), and refuses the same malformed bodies the save path refuses.
