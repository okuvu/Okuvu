## From the same PDF — safety plan, items 2–7

2. Things I can do on my own to get through it

List. Prompt: What helps you, even a little, when you're by yourself? Each item can link
to an OKUVU exercise (for example 5-4-3-2-1 grounding or a breathing exercise).

3. People and places that help take my mind off it

List. Prompt: Who or where can you be around, without having to talk about how you
feel?

4. People I can ask for help

List with name and phone number, each with a one-tap call button. Prompt: Who could
you tell that you're struggling?

5. Professional and crisis support

Therapist's contact details, entered by the therapist, plus the verified helpline list. The
helpline list is pulled from the app's central helpline config and must be checked
before every release. It is not typed into each plan.

6. Making my surroundings safer

List. Prompt: What can you do to make your space safer during a hard moment?
Worded in session with the client.

7. One reason that matters to me

Short text, optional. Prompt: What's something worth holding on to?

No closing line. The plan always ends with a fixed Get help now button that opens the
crisis screen.

## From `DOC-20261109-WA0028..md` — Before & after session

### 14. What I want to bring to session

Self-serve · Low load · 3 min · For therapists: client-led agenda setting. If shared, it appears in the session-prep digest.

Intro: "Sessions can go by fast, and things slip your mind. Jot down what you'd like to talk about, so it's ready when you need it."

1. **What's been on your mind since last session?**

Long text.

2. **Is there anything you've been finding hard to say out loud?**

Long text, optional. Helper: "You can write it here first. You decide whether to share it."

3. **If you could leave next session with one thing, what would it be?**

Short text, optional. Examples: "A plan for exam week" · "Just feeling heard."

4. **Share with your therapist before the session?**

Choice: "Share" · "Keep private". Both buttons are the same size and neither is pre-selected.

Closing line: "Ready when you are."
