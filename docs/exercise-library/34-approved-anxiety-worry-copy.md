## Worry time (`ws_worry_time`)

assigned · light · 10 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Worrying has a way of spreading across the whole day. Giving it one time and one place doesn't make it disappear — it gives it somewhere to wait.

- **What time each day do you want to give it?**
  > Pick a time when you can usually stop for a bit. It doesn't have to be perfect, and you can move it later.
- **How long do you want?**
  > A shorter window is easier to keep. You can always finish early.
  - 10 minutes
  - 15 minutes
  - 20 minutes
- **What's parked for that time?**
  > During the day, when a worry turns up, put it here instead of carrying it round. When your worry time comes, come back and look at each one.
- **What do you notice about the ones you parked?**
  > Optional. A word is enough.

Closing: **A worry kept for later is a worry you're not carrying all day.**

## Brain-dump (`ws_brain_dump`)

open · light · 5 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Nothing here has to be tidy. Put the whole swirl down in one place, and let it be as messy as it actually is.

- **What's in your head right now?**
  > One long list is fine. Nothing has to be in order. You can type this or say it out loud — whichever is easier.
- **What would you like to do with it?**
  - Save it
    > It goes into Past me, private unless you choose to share it.
  - Let it go
    > This deletes it straight away. If OKUVU already checked in about something you wrote, your therapist may still know that a check-in happened.

Closing: **You got it out of your head. That's the whole job.**

## Unhooking from a thought (`ws_unhooking`)

assigned · light · 10 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> A thought can feel like a fact about the world. Saying it back as a thought, in your own words, helps it sound like what it is: a thought.

- **What's the thought that keeps coming?**
  > Write it the way it arrives, exactly as harsh as it sounds. Nothing here is edited.
- **How much do you believe it right now?**
  - 0 = not at all
  - 100 = completely
- **Here it is again, a little further away.**
  > These are your own words. Nothing has been changed.
  > I'm having the thought that …
  > I notice I'm having the thought that …
- **And now, having read it back?**
  - 0 = not at all
  - 100 = completely
- **What's different, if anything, about the thought now?**
  > Optional. Nothing has to have changed.

Closing: **The thought didn't have to change for you to step back from it.**

## Sitting with not knowing (`ws_sitting_with_not_knowing`)

assigned · light · 5 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Some things don't have an answer yet. Trying to force one can keep you stuck. This is a few minutes of letting a question stay open.

- **What are you unsure about?**
  > A few words. You don't have to explain the whole situation.
- **How uncomfortable does leaving it unknown feel?**
  - 0 = not at all
  - 10 = very hard
- **One small way you'll let it stay unknown today**
  > Something you can actually do, or actually not do. "I'll wait until Thursday before deciding" counts. Checking it one more time doesn't.
- **And now, having written that?**
  - 0 = not at all
  - 10 = very hard
- **What do you notice?**
  > Optional.

Closing: **Not knowing for a while is not the same as giving up.**

## Big day planner (`ws_exam_career_pressure`)

open · light · 10 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Big days take up a lot of room in your head. Putting the practical parts somewhere helps you keep your head for the day itself.

- **When is it?**
  > The date is enough.
- **What needs doing before then?**
  > One step per line, with the day you will do it. Small steps are easier to keep.
- **On the day**
  > Tick these as they happen. Nothing here is a rule.
  - eat something before I go
  - leave earlier than I need to
  - take what I need with me
  - put my phone on silent
  - get there and sit down for a minute
  - plan something kind for afterwards
- **What do you want to remember on the day?**
  > One sentence you'd want to read at the door.
  > Example: I've done the work. I just have to turn up.

Closing: **The day will go how it goes. You'll have done what you could.**

## Social situation prep (`ws_social_situation_prep`)

assigned · light · 10 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Before something social, the mind often writes the ending in advance. This is somewhere to write that ending down — and then, later, what actually happened.

- **What's the situation?**
  > A few words is enough.
  > Example: Drinks with the team on Friday.
- **What do you think will happen?**
  > The version your mind keeps showing you, in its own words.
- **How worried do you feel about it?**
  - 0 = calm
  - 10 = very worried
- **One small thing you'll try**
  > Small is the point. Say one sentence. Stay ten minutes. Arrive and leave when you like.
- **If you have done it — how did it go?**
  > Leave this until afterwards. You can come back to this worksheet.
  - Went as feared
  - Better than feared
  - Didn't do it
- **What actually happened?**
  > Just what happened, in plain words.
- **What got in the way?**
  > No judgement. This helps you and your therapist plan better.

Closing: **A prediction written down is a prediction you can check.**

## Pressure map (`ws_family_expectations`)

assigned · light · 10 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> A lot of pressure comes from what we think other people expect. Mapping it shows which of it is yours, and which you picked up somewhere else.

- **Which areas are carrying expectations?**
  > One line for each area that applies — studies, career, money, family, marriage, friends, the future. Skip the ones that carry nothing.
- **What do you notice, seeing them side by side?**
  > Optional.

Closing: **Naming whose expectation it is doesn't settle it. It does make it easier to see.**

## Exposure ladder (`ws_exposure_ladder`)

therapist_only · demanding · 20 min · care plan: Anxiety & Worry

Intro:

> Your therapist builds this ladder with you, in session. Each step is added together, and unlocked when you are both ready for it.

- **You will see only the steps that have been unlocked for you. For each one, rate how difficult it feels before you try it, and again afterwards. If a step feels like too much on the day, it can wait.**
- **Before you try this step, how difficult does it feel?**
  - 0 = not difficult
  - 100 = as difficult as it gets
- **And afterwards?**
  - 0 = not difficult
  - 100 = as difficult as it gets

Closing: **Each step is yours to take when you are ready.**
