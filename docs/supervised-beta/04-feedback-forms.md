## 1. Therapist feedback — structured form (required)

Created from these questions verbatim. All ratings are 1–5 (1 = poor, 5 = excellent).

1. **T-01 Dashboard** — how easy was it to see your caseload, care plans, and statuses?
2. **T-02 Client overview** — did the session summary and safety indicator give you
   what you needed to supervise? (rating + open field)
3. **T-03 Care plan change** — how easy was it to change/confirm a client's care plan?
4. **T-05 Session notes** — how easy was it to write and find your notes?
5. **Portal usability overall** — rating + open field "what was confusing or missing?"
6. **Session workflow** — did reviewing the summary and the `reasoning_note` help you
   supervise sessions?
7. **A2 content quality observations** — free text: any turns that seemed clinically
   off-target, abrupt, or inappropriate? Quote the turn if you can.
   > Note (shown to therapist): content you judge **clinically inappropriate** must be
   > escalated as a safety concern (E2), not only reported here.
8. **Safety concerns** — did any client show distress or a crisis moment? Was the crisis
   pathway correct? Any near-miss you want recorded?
9. **Session latency / technical issues** — any slowness, disconnects, or crashes on the
   client's side you observed or heard about?
10. **What would you change?** — free text.
11. **Would you keep this client in Evia after the beta?** — Yes / No / Not sure.

### Response handling

- One submission per therapist; confirm coverage in the participant roster.
- A2 content quality comments feed E13-S04 triage; anything clinically inappropriate is
  an E2 escalation per `01-escalation-plan.md`.

## 2. Client feedback — short form (encouraged, optional)

1. **Overall experience** — how would you rate your sessions with Evia? (1–5)
2. **Did you feel heard?** — (1–5)
3. **Any moment of discomfort or concern?** — free text
4. **What would make Evia better for you?** — free text

## 3. Completion rule

The beta is not complete until the therapist form is collected for **every**
participating therapist. Client forms are collected opportunistically and summarized
in the completion report.
