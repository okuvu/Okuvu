# Therapist Walkthrough Guide

> **⚠️ Dependency:** E13-S01 dogfooding must be completed and all P0 portal issues
> resolved **before** any walkthrough sessions are scheduled. The walkthrough covers
> features that must be verified stable through internal dogfooding first.

## Session overview

- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes per therapist
- **Format:** In-person or video call with screen sharing
- **Facilitator:** Lead Developer or product PM
- **Prerequisites:** Therapist account seeded, therapist signed in on their own device

## Agenda

### 1. T-00: Sign in & orientation (5 min)

**Facilitator actions:**
- Confirm therapist can sign in via Google OAuth or phone OTP
- Confirm therapist sees the sidebar with "Dashboard" highlighted
- Point out the "Patients" section in the sidebar
- Note the "Evia — Therapist Portal" branding

**Key points to emphasize:**
- The portal is the therapist's interface to their clients' Evia activity
- All client data is encrypted and access-controlled
- Walk them through the sidebar: Dashboard, Schedule (coming soon), Safety Reviews (coming soon), Reports (coming soon)

### 2. T-01: Dashboard & caseload (5 min)

**Facilitator actions:**
- Show therapist their caseload on the dashboard
- Point out each column: Client, Care Plan, Status, Last Session, Safety
- Click a client name or "View" to navigate to client detail

**Key points to emphasize:**
- The dashboard shows all clients assigned to this therapist
- Care Plan shows which track the client is on (CP1/CP2/CP3)
- Status shows whether the assignment is Provisional or Confirmed
- Safety column flags clients needing review (red indicator)

### 3. T-02: Client overview (15 min)

**Facilitator actions:**
- Navigate to a specific client's detail page
- Walk through each section:

  **Client Overview:**
  - Phone number (masked)
  - Safety state indicator

  **Care Plan:**
  - Current care plan name
  - Assignment state (Provisional or Confirmed) — **VERBAL EMPHASIS HERE**
  - "Manage" link to change care plan

  **Clinical Reasoning (if present):**
  - This is the `reasoning_note` — **VERBAL EMPHASIS HERE**
  - Explain what it is and what it is NOT

  **Latest Session Summary:**
  - Shows the most recent session summary
  - May show "generating" if still processing

  **Session Notes link:**
  - Leads to T-05

**Key concepts to explain — provisional vs confirmed:**

| Aspect | Provisional | Confirmed |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Meaning | System-suggested based on initial onboarding | Reviewed and set by a therapist |
| Who set it | The A2 agent (AI) | The therapist manually |
| Stability | May change as more data comes in | Stable until therapist changes it |
| When to act | Review and confirm if appropriate | No action needed |

**Key concepts to explain — reasoning_note:**

- The `reasoning_note` is generated by the A2 agent during onboarding
- It is the system's internal inference about why a care plan was suggested
- **It is NEVER shown to the client** — this is a deliberate clinical boundary
- It is visible to therapists only, for clinical review purposes
- It may contain notes about specific client responses during onboarding
- It is informational, not prescriptive — the therapist makes the final decision

**Common misconception to address:**
The `reasoning_note` is NOT a recommendation to the therapist. It is the system's onboarding
inference, provided for transparency so the therapist understands why the system suggested
a particular care plan.

### 4. T-03: Care plan assignment (10 min)

**Facilitator actions:**
- Click "Manage" on the care plan card (or navigate to `/clients/[id]/program`)
- Show the current care plan with its assignment state badge
- Show the care plan selector dropdown (CP1/CP2/CP3)
- Change the care plan and save

**Key points to emphasize:**
- Changing a care plan automatically sets `assignment_state` to `confirmed`
- This means: once you explicitly set a care plan, the system trusts your judgment
- The system will not auto-change a confirmed assignment
- This matters clinically: a confirmed care plan means the therapist has made an intentional decision
- The "Notice" box: notes are not used by Evia (reinforces T-05 lesson)

**When to change a care plan:**
- Client's primary concern shifts (e.g., from anxiety to stress)
- Initial onboarding assignment doesn't fit
- Clinical judgment indicates a different track

**When NOT to change a care plan:**
- Based on a single session's mood
- Without reviewing the reasoning note
- Without consulting the client (clinical best practice)

### 5. T-05: Session notes & privacy (10 min)

**Facilitator actions:**
- Navigate to Session Notes via the link on T-02
- Show the privacy notice at the top
- Write a sample note and save it
- Show that the note appears in the history

**Key points to emphasize — the non-ingestion design:**

- **Session notes are NEVER read by any Evia AI agent**
- They are stored securely in the database
- They are visible to the therapist and only the therapist
- They do not influence the AI's responses, care plan suggestions, or any other automated behavior
- This is a deliberate architectural boundary: the therapist controls what informs the AI

**What session notes ARE for:**
- Your private clinical notes during sessions
- Reminders to yourself between sessions
- Observations you want to track over time
- Anything clinically relevant you want to document

**What session notes are NOT for:**
- They are NOT instructions to the AI
- They are NOT care plan configuration
- They are NOT shared with the client
- They are NOT ingested for training or improvement

**If a therapist asks "but what if I want the AI to know something?":**
Direct them to the care plan selector (T-03). The care plan is how therapists
configure what the AI focuses on. Session notes are the therapist's private workspace.

### 6. End-of-session navigation task (5-10 min)

**The therapist performs unaided:**

1. Sign in to the portal (if not already signed in)
2. Navigate to the dashboard
3. Find a specific client (provide client ID or phone number)
4. Navigate to that client's detail page (T-02)
5. Describe to the facilitator:
   - Whether their care plan is `provisional` or `confirmed`
   - What `reasoning_note` means (in their own words)
   - Whether session notes are read by the AI

**Pass criteria:**
- Correctly identifies `assignment_state` as provisional or confirmed
- Can articulate that `reasoning_note` is the system's onboarding inference, visible only to therapists, never shown to the client
- States clearly that session notes are NOT read by the AI

**Fail criteria (requires follow-up session):**
- Confuses provisional and confirmed assignment meanings
- Thinks `reasoning_note` is shown to the client
- Describes session notes as "feeding into the AI"

## After the session

1. Fill out the session notes template within 24 hours
2. If therapist passed the navigation task, fill out the beta clearance checklist
3. If therapist failed, schedule a follow-up walkthrough session
4. Log any UX issues discovered using the UX issue logging template
5. Share walkthrough notes with the team

## Notes on portal maturity

The T-02 (client detail) and T-05 (session notes) screens are the initial
implementation built as part of E13-S02. They cover the core workflows described
in this guide. If E11-S04 or a later story adds richer interactions (e.g.,
filtering notes, inline editing, additional client fields), update this guide
to reflect those enhancements.

All backend APIs used by the walkthrough (`clients.detail`, `notes.addNote`,
`notes.listNotes`) are stable and independently tested.

## Tips for facilitators

- Let the therapist drive the mouse — screen sharing is better than demo
- Encourage questions — confusion now prevents problems later
- If a concept is confusing, try a different explanation rather than repeating
- Take note of which parts were confusing for improving future walkthroughs
- The navigation task should feel like a natural check, not a test
