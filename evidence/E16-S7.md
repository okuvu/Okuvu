## Honest limits of this evidence

1. **One deletion, one row-set.** The story asks for it to be exercised once; it was exercised once, on a
   synthetic client in the live beta database. No seeded therapist and no real client row was read,
   modified or deleted (E24 vs E30).
2. **The client-side UI was not clicked.** Deletion is invoked from the mobile app, which is not installed
   here; the button's effect is `POST /account/delete`, which is what was called — with a real token, on
   the deployed service (E27). The UI itself is unverified.
3. **`TherapistClient` rows are the join between a client and a therapist**; deletion leaves them, so the
   therapist's dashboard keeps a link to a client whose account row is a tombstone. Observed, not
   inferred (E28).
4. **No CloudTrail/data-event audit of secrets access** was performed (permissions: `cloudfront`/
   `apigateway` were already denied to this role, E4). "Secrets are not read by anything unexpected" is
   therefore not proven.
5. **The Google OAuth credential in E11 was not tested against Google** — revoking it needs GCP access this
   host does not have. It is reported as "present in history, no evidence of revocation", not as dead.
6. **Log scanning is a 14-day window** — equal to the retention of the three application groups, so for
   those three it is the whole history that exists. The never-expiring groups (E17) were sampled, not
   exhaustively scanned.
