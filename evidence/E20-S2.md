## 5. What could **not** be verified

1. **Clinical-owner approval.** Structurally impossible for this branch. §6.6 is blank; the banner is
   the deliverable. This is the one unmet clause of E20-S2.
2. **The true clinical authority's identity and contact.** `plan/ORCHESTRATION-v1.md` §6 names
   "Shubhangi" as the clinical authority for E17-S7; the supervised-beta escalation plan's contact table
   (`docs/supervised-beta/01-escalation-plan.md` §2) has `—` for every role. The runbook therefore
   routes through "the clinical owner, via the ops contact sheet" rather than printing a personal
   address into a public repository.
3. **Whether the SNS email subscription was confirmed after this run.** It was `PendingConfirmation` at
   15:37Z. Only the mailbox owner can click the link.
4. **The Bedrock quota value.** `servicequotas:ListServiceQuotas` is denied to the `keel-deploy` role
   (error quoted in §3). §3.3 sends the reader to the console page and states the denial rather than
   guessing a number.
5. **Whether the leaked Google OAuth client is already revoked.** No GCP credentials in this
   environment; the repo only shows that the value was committed and removed. §5.2 says "treat it as
   live until the console says otherwise".
6. **`EstimatedTPMQuotaUsage` semantics as a throttling predictor.** AWS's own documentation says it is
   an estimate of *post-completion* consumption and must not be the sole indicator, because throttling
   is decided on the upfront `input + max_tokens` reservation. The runbook repeats that caveat and
   treats the application log line as authoritative.
7. **Restore-over-production, PITR, and restore at real data volume.** Not drilled, by design. §4.4.
8. **A live rollback.** Not performed — rolling a production service back to prove the procedure would
   itself be an incident. The commands were verified as commands (argument shapes, derived values,
   service names, the confirmation query against a real PRIMARY deployment); the data plane was not
   exercised.
9. **A live secret rotation.** Not performed: rotating the production database password to prove the
   procedure would take the stack down if any step were wrong. Everything except the mutating calls was
   verified (secret contents shape, URL rebuild dry-run, task-definition `secrets` references, the
   in-VPC probe script's existence and interface).
10. **SNS→email delivery.** Cannot be tested without confirming the subscription on the owner's behalf.
