## Acceptance criterion

> clinical_content and tone_alliance reach their stated thresholds on the
> E17-S5 v2 set — anhedonia, grief, burnout and health anxiety each receive a
> technique other than a breathing exercise, no reply endorses paper-bag
> rebreathing, and a one-word reply is not answered with re-onboarding.

## What changed

A2 prompt **v2.1 → v2.2** (`src/prompts/a2-okuvu-v2.2.txt`), new §7.1: exactly
one concrete, do-able-in-two-minutes step per distress reply, chosen from a
presentation table. The table names the failure modes explicitly — anhedonia
gets behavioural activation at the smallest scale and is told *not* a breath;
panic is told paper-bag rebreathing is forbidden; grief is told "closure" and
"time heals" are forbidden; health anxiety is told not to reassure the fear away
or contradict the doctor.

`A2_PROMPT_VERSION` bumped so a stored turn's `prompt_version` identifies which
file wrote it, and the a2 test now asserts against the constant rather than a
literal so the two cannot drift.

## Measured result — NOT met

Three full v2 gate runs, 369 judgements each, against real Bedrock on the api
task's own environment (`qwen.qwen3-32b-v1:0`, ap-south-1):

| run | clinical_content | tone_alliance | overall |
|-----|------------------|---------------|---------|
| baseline (v2.1, `evidence/E17-S5-v2-runs/`) | 60.6% | 72.2% | 73.7% |
| r1 (v2.2, before overlay fixes) | 80.3% | 68.5% | 84.0% |
| r2 (v2.2 + overlays) | 77.3% | 72.2% | 87.8% |
| r3 (v2.2 + overlays) | 78.8% | 72.2% | 86.7% |
| **threshold** | **85%** | **85%** | **90%** |

So: clinical_content improved materially — roughly 60% → 78-80% — which is the
part §7.1 was written for. tone_alliance did **not** improve: 72.2% before,
72.2% after. The overall gate moved 73.7% → ~87% but is still short of 90%.

**The story is not met and is not claimed to be.** The criterion names two
thresholds; one improved and one did not move.

## What the remaining failures look like

tone_alliance is dominated by ta-03 and its neighbours: a one-word reply
("okay") answered by restarting the offer instead of continuing it. §3.1 of the
prompt already forbids this and the model does it anyway, which suggests the
rule is in tension with the "always validate first" golden rule rather than
missing. That is a prompt-structure problem, not a missing paragraph, and it
needs a different approach than §7.1 — most likely an explicit worked example
for the one-word case rather than another rule.

clinical_content remaining failures are spread rather than concentrated, which
is what a 78% score looks like: different scenarios fail on different runs. The
honest read is that the judge is scoring a stochastic 32B model at temperature
0.7 against 22 distinct clinical requirements, and a single prompt revision does
not close a 15-point gap on that.

  test.

## Recommendation

Do not dispatch a fourth single-shot prompt revision against these numbers.
tone_alliance needs the one-word/continuation case fixed structurally, and
clinical_content needs either a larger gold set with the same thresholds (so a
78% is reported as 78% rather than as a near-miss) or a decision that 0.85 is the
wrong bar for a 32B model at 0.7. Both are judgement calls about rigour and
budget, and both belong to the plan owner, not to whoever runs the next wave.
